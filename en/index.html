The notebook’s rules

Six rules, one wheel

Turn the wheel or tap a slice; the rule under the pointer opens beside it.

  1. 01 / 06

    sentiment_satisfied

    01

    A playful tone

    Notes are written to make you smile; they never scare anyone.

  2. 02 / 06

    block

    02

    No definite forecasts

    We write “could” instead of “will”; the future is written in no one’s notebook.

  3. 03 / 06

    short_text

    03

    Short and clear

    One sentence per sign; no long, confusing readings.

  4. 04 / 06

    health_and_safety

    04

    Not advice

    Ask an expert about health, legal or money matters; the notebook is only interpretation.

  5. 05 / 06

    public

    05

    The real sky

    Dates like equinoxes and solstices come from astronomy sources.

  6. 06 / 06

    lock

    06

    Your message stays yours

    Reader messages are used only to reply and are never published.
